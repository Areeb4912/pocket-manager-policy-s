Privacy Policy

Pocket Manager

Effective date: November 3, 2026 · Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Manager handles information when you use the app. Pocket Manager is a personal expense and income tracking tool. The current app version is designed to keep the records you enter on the device where you use the app.

In brief: The current app code stores your transactions, settings, and calculator history in local device/browser storage. It does not send those records to a Pocket Manager server. There is no account sign-in or cloud-sync feature in the current version.

1. Information you enter

Depending on the features you use, Pocket Manager may store information you choose to enter, including:

  • Transaction details such as amounts, dates, income or expense type, category, payment method, and descriptions or notes.
  • Recurring transaction rules and custom categories.
  • App preferences such as currency, manually entered exchange rates, and display settings.
  • Calculator history.
  • PIN-lock settings, if you choose to enable the optional PIN feature.

Please do not enter bank passwords, card security codes, full payment-card numbers, or other credentials. Pocket Manager is not a bank and does not connect to your bank account.

2. Where information is stored

The current version stores app information in local browser/app storage on your device. The app code uses browser local storage for transaction data and calculator history. The developer does not receive these records through an app-operated server, and the current version does not provide account-based cloud storage or synchronization.

Information stored locally may be accessible to someone who can access your unlocked device or its app/browser data. The optional PIN lock is described in the app as basic protection only; it is not strong security and does not encrypt your records. Use your device's own screen lock and keep your device secure.

3. Backups, imports, and exports

You can choose to create a JSON backup, export transaction information as a CSV file, or export a report as a PDF. These files are created for you to save on your device or another location you choose. You can also choose a JSON backup file to restore data in the app. If you independently share, upload, or store an exported file with another person or service, that recipient or service will handle the file under its own terms and privacy practices. Pocket Manager does not receive a copy merely because you export it.

4. Information sharing

The current app version does not sell your transaction information or share it with advertisers. The current app code does not include an app-operated analytics or advertising service. Because your app records are stored locally and are not sent to a Pocket Manager server, the developer does not routinely receive or disclose those records.

If you use the website version hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process ordinary technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as connection and request information, under GitHub's own privacy terms. If you email the support address, your email address and the content of your message will be processed by the email provider and by the developer to respond to you.

Your Android device, Google Play, and any services you choose to use for storing or sharing exported files may process information under their own privacy policies. Those services are separate from Pocket Manager.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your app records remain in local app/browser storage until you delete them or remove the app's stored data. You can use the app's Settings → Clear All Data option to remove the records stored by the app. You can also clear the app's storage through your device or browser settings. Exported CSV and JSON files are separate copies; delete those files from each location where you saved or shared them.

If you contact support by email, the support correspondence may be retained in the support inbox for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and keep support records, then deleted when no longer needed, subject to applicable requirements.

6. Security

Pocket Manager is designed for local use, but no method of storage is completely secure. Local browser/app storage is not a substitute for encrypted financial record storage. The optional PIN is a basic convenience lock, not a guarantee against access by someone with control of your device. Keep your device and any exported backup files secure.

7. Children's privacy

Pocket Manager does not ask users to create an account or provide their name, email address, or date of birth to use its local tracking features. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the support email, please contact us so we can review and delete the message where appropriate.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be made available at the public URL where this policy is hosted. The effective date at the top indicates when it was last updated.
